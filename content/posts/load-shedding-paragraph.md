<p>Load shedding is one of the most common paragraph topics in school exams in Bangladesh. Students from Class 6 to Class 10 often get it in the English paper, and the question usually asks for a fixed number of words. So here you will find three versions on one page: <strong>100 words</strong>, <strong>150 words</strong> and <strong>250 words</strong>. Pick the one that matches your class and your exam instructions.</p>

<h2>Load Shedding Paragraph in 100 Words (Class 6-7)</h2>

<p>Load-shedding means cutting off electricity for some time in an area. It happens when the demand for power is higher than the supply. In our country, it is a common problem, especially in summer. When the current goes off, we cannot use fans, lights or computers. Students find it hard to study at night. Shops and small factories suffer badly. Patients in hospitals face trouble. The main causes are the shortage of gas and fuel, old power plants and poor distribution. We should save electricity and use solar power. The government should build more power plants to end this problem.</p>

<h2>Load Shedding Paragraph in 150 Words (Class 8)</h2>

<p>Load-shedding has almost become a part of our daily life. It means a temporary stop of electricity supply in an area. It happens when the demand for power is higher than the amount we produce. The main causes are the shortage of gas and fuel, old power plants, and poor distribution lines. Load-shedding creates many problems for ordinary people like us. Students cannot study properly in the evening, and many of them fail to finish their homework. Shops, factories and hospitals also suffer because they cannot work without electricity. It occurs mostly in the hot summer, when people use more fans and air conditioners. Power is cut off area by area to balance the load on the national grid. We can reduce this problem by building new power plants, saving electricity and using solar energy. Both the government and the people must take proper steps to solve this serious problem soon.</p>

<h2>Load Shedding Paragraph in 250 Words (Class 9-10)</h2>

<p>Load-shedding is a serious problem in Bangladesh. It means a planned and temporary break in the supply of electricity to some areas. When the demand for power becomes higher than what the power plants can produce, the authority has to cut the supply area by area. This keeps the national grid safe from a total blackout.</p>

<p>There are several reasons behind it. The shortage of gas and imported fuel is the biggest one. Many power plants are old and cannot run at full capacity. Poor transmission and distribution lines waste a part of the electricity before it reaches the people. In summer, the demand rises sharply because people use fans, refrigerators and air conditioners at the same time.</p>

<p>Load-shedding affects every sector of life. Students cannot read or do their homework after dark, and this harms their exam results. Farmers cannot run irrigation pumps in time. Factories stop production, so owners lose money and workers lose their jobs. Hospitals face serious trouble when an operation is going on. Small shops also lose their customers. Sleep is also disturbed at night.</p>

<p>However, we can reduce this problem if we take the right steps. The government should build new power plants and make better use of solar, wind and other renewable sources. It should repair the old lines and stop the waste of power. On the other hand, each of us must switch off unused lights and fans. If everyone plays a part, we can hope for a brighter future without load-shedding.</p>

<h2>Load Shedding Paragraph: Questions and Answers (a-f)</h2>

<p>Many textbooks ask you to write the paragraph by answering a few questions. Here are short answers you can use as a guide.</p>

<h3>(a) What do you mean by load-shedding?</h3>
<p>Load-shedding means stopping the electricity supply in an area for some time because the demand is more than the supply.</p>

<h3>(b) What problems does it create?</h3>
<p>It disturbs study, business, farming, factory work and hospital services. It also makes daily life uncomfortable, especially in the heat.</p>

<h3>(c) How does it affect the students?</h3>
<p>Students cannot study or finish their homework at night. This can hurt their preparation and their results.</p>

<h3>(d) When does it occur?</h3>
<p>It occurs mostly in the evening and in the summer season, when the use of electricity goes up.</p>

<h3>(e) How does it occur?</h3>
<p>When the power plants cannot meet the demand, the authority switches off the supply to one area after another to keep the grid stable.</p>

<h3>(f) How can we stop it?</h3>
<p>We can stop it by building more power plants, using solar and other renewable energy, repairing the old lines and saving electricity in our homes.</p>

<h2>Tips for Writing a Paragraph in the Exam</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Check the word limit first. Write close to it, not far above or below.</li>
  <li>Start with a clear first sentence that tells what load-shedding is.</li>
  <li>Cover causes, effects and solutions in that order. It keeps the paragraph smooth.</li>
  <li>Write in a single block. A paragraph should not be broken into points.</li>
  <li>Use simple sentences you are sure about. Spelling mistakes cost more marks than short sentences.</li>
  <li>Do not memorize blindly. Learn the ideas, then write them in your own words.</li>
</ul>
